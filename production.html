What This Actually Is

This Is Not "Extra Classes"

This is a structured, 12-month collegiate preparation system built around three pillars.

🩰

1. Technical & Athletic Mastery

We build the physical tools collegiate programs expect. Technical weaknesses are addressed early — not ignored.

  • ✦ Ballet foundation
  • ✦ Advanced turns & leap progression
  • ✦ Kick stamina & pom precision
  • ✦ Collegiate-style hip hop
  • ✦ Tumble training (with former Level 12 gymnast)
🏟️

2. Collegiate Performance Readiness

We train dancers to function inside a team environment. This is preparation for collegiate standards — not recital performance.

  • ✦ Game day simulations
  • ✦ Pom synchronization
  • ✦ Performance under pressure
  • ✦ Quarterly performance projects
  • ✦ Real-world exposure opportunities
🎓

3. Recruitment & Professional Development

We remove audition shock. We do not guess at recruitment. We prepare for it.

  • ✦ Three Mock Tryouts annually
  • ✦ Written evaluations & scoring
  • ✦ Interview preparation
  • ✦ Portfolio & recruitment video guidance
  • ✦ College matching support
  • ✦ Parent education nights
