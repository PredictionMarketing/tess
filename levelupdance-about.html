About Level Up Dance

Built by a Coach Who's
Been in the Arena

Level Up Dance was founded by Tess Liantonio — a former D1 head coach, professional dancer, and choreographer who has worked at the highest levels of performance in both collegiate athletics and the entertainment industry.

Your Coach

Tess Liantonio

Founder & Head Coach — Level Up Dance

Tess is a professional dancer, choreographer, and the founder of Level Up Dance, based out of Charlotte, North Carolina. She brings an unmatched combination of collegiate coaching experience and professional entertainment credits to every class she teaches.

As the Head Coach of the UNC Charlotte dance team for five seasons, Tess evaluated hundreds of collegiate auditions, built competitive programs, and developed deep relationships with over 250 college dance staffs nationwide. She knows exactly what coaches are looking for — because she was one.

Beyond the collegiate world, Tess has performed and choreographed for some of the most recognizable brands in entertainment. Her credits include work on Netflix, Hallmark Channel, Bvlgari, MAC Cosmetics, the Today Show, and more.

Level Up Dance is her vision for a studio that doesn't just teach dance — it teaches dancers how to succeed. Whether a student is preparing for a college dance team tryout or building a foundation for professional performance, Tess and her faculty deliver training that is purposeful, structured, and always connected to real-world results.

Our Mission

More Than a Dance Studio

Level Up Dance exists to close the gap between where dancers are and where they want to be. We don't believe in shortcuts — we believe in systems, structure, and the right coaching.

"Talent wins auditions. Preparation wins careers."

Every class, every evaluation, and every mentoring session at Level Up is designed to move dancers closer to their goals — whether that's a college dance team roster, a professional gig, or simply becoming the most complete dancer they can be.

🎯 Intentional Training

Every session has a purpose. We don't do filler — we build skills methodically, with clear progressions and benchmarks.

💡 Real-World Readiness

We train for what actually happens in auditions, tryouts, and performances — not hypothetical scenarios.

🤝 Access & Connections

Tess's network of 250+ college dance staffs gives our dancers insight and introductions that others simply don't have.

🔥 High Standards, Real Support

We push hard and we care deeply. This isn't a casual rec program — but it is a supportive, growth-focused environment.

✨ Joy in the Craft

Dance should be challenging and fun. We create an atmosphere where hard work and genuine enjoyment coexist.
