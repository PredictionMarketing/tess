The Structure
This is a 12-month collegiate preparation system built around three pillars:
1. Technical & Athletic Mastery
Ballet foundation
Advanced turns and leap progression
Kick stamina and pom precision
Collegiate-style hip hop
Tumble training (with former Level 12 gymnast)
Weaknesses are addressed early.
Standards are raised steadily.

2. Collegiate Performance Readiness
Game day simulation
Performance under fatigue
Synchronization and uniformity
Quarterly performance projects
Real-world exposure
We train dancers to function inside a team — not just perform solos.

3. Recruitment & Professional Development
3 Mock Tryouts annually
Written scoring and feedback
Interview preparation
Portfolio and reel guidance
College matching support
Parent education sessions
Auditions should feel familiar — not overwhelming.
Preparation replaces guesswork.

The Culture
Collegiate programs recruit for skill.
They retain for character.
In this track, dancers are expected to:
Support teammates
Accept correction
Maintain professionalism
Contribute positively to the room
Growth is not rushed.
It is layered.
Correction is not criticism.
It is investment.
Love in this studio looks like structure.
It looks like accountability.
It looks like long-term development over short-term applause.

The Outcome
By senior year, dancers in this track should:
Execute advanced skills consistently
Maintain stamina under pressure
Perform multiple collegiate styles confidently
Interview with clarity and maturity
Understand recruitment timelines
Evaluate program fit strategically
Placement cannot be guaranteed.
Preparation can.

Enrollment

Cohort size is intentionally limited to protect:

Technical integrity
Studio culture
This is not a seasonal add-on.
