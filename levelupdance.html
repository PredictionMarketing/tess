The Reality

Getting on a College Team
Doesn't Start Senior Year

It's layered. It's structured. It rewards consistency. Waiting doesn't create advantage — training does.

📆

Timing Matters

Collegiate recruitment is a multi-year process. The dancers who make teams started preparing early — not cramming at the last minute.

🎯

Skills Are Specific

College teams audition for specific techniques: turns, leaps, pom precision, game-day performance. General dance training isn't enough.

🔑

Connections Open Doors

Knowing what each program values — and having introductions to staff — gives you an edge that talent alone can't.
